我想去台北車站，從這裡搭計程車過去大約需要多少錢呢?
I want to go to Taipei Main Station. How much would a taxi ride from here cost approximately?
從集思台大會議中心搭乘計程車前往臺北車站，車程距離約為 4.8 公里，在順暢情況下預計耗時約 15 分鐘。預估車資大約在 NT$ 180 ～ NT$ 230 之間（依實際行駛路線、停等紅綠燈時間與塞車狀況而定）。
Taking a taxi from the GIS NTU Conference Center to Taipei Main Station is approximately 4.8 kilometers and should take about 15 minutes under normal traffic conditions. The estimated fare is between NT$180 and NT$230 (depending on the actual route, time spent at traffic lights, and traffic conditions).
